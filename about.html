About Me

My academic experience is complemented by a 16-month professional internship, where I worked hands-on with technical hardware and software, managed real-world projects, and developed efficient, goal-driven plans.

I am eager to continue advancing my skills through graduate studies, contributing to research and innovation in the field of audio technology.

Outside of academics, I'm a multi-instrumentalist, singer, songwriter, and producer. I've released two musical projects (an EP and an album), both fully self-recorded, produced, and mixed.
